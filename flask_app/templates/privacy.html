Saved when enabled

Check-in and reviewed information

If you enable approved check-in saving, completed check-in information and reviewed results may be stored with your account for history and Personal Pattern Analysis.

  • Your structured question responses
  • Today's Check-In Status
  • Question, Reflection and Combined wellbeing scores
  • Reviewed text-emotion suggestions
  • Supportive factors and areas of strain
  • Vocal-pattern observations when available
  • Visible-expression observations when available
  • Your confirmed personal summary
  • Confirmed appointment discussion points
  • Resources you bookmark

A reflection-only entry does not create a Today's Check-In Status because that status requires the four core questions.

Temporary only

Not permanently saved

Reflection media and processing files are used only while the reflection is being processed.

  • Raw voice recordings
  • Raw video recordings
  • Extracted audio
  • Sampled video frames and face crops
  • Voice and video transcripts
  • Temporary media-processing files
